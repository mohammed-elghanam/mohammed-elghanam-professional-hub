## Full description

Mohammed Elghanam R&D Hub is a professional executive portfolio for engineering leadership, product development, innovation and technical consulting.

Explore a structured profile covering more than 25 years of R&D and embedded-systems experience across smart metering, IoT, LPWAN, FPGA, industrial automation, power-grid protection and safety-critical systems.

The app brings together:

- Executive professional profile and leadership positioning
- Selected engineering project case studies
- Embedded systems, IoT, FPGA and industrial expertise
- Career and executive CV information
- Five granted patents and innovation highlights
- Awards and professional recognition gallery
- International events, smart-utilities showcases and railway-safety training
- Built-in executive PDF document center
- PDF viewing, zooming, saving and sharing
- Professional contact links, LinkedIn and universal web hub

Selected project areas include smart utilities and smart metering, LPWAN IoT platforms, embedded EMS and RTU systems, power-grid protection, medical-device R&D, mission-critical communication platforms and railway level-crossing systems.

The application is designed as a focused professional portfolio for executives, engineering leaders, technical partners, clients, investors, recruiters and organizations seeking collaboration in R&D, embedded product development, IoT and advanced engineering systems.

No account or registration is required.
