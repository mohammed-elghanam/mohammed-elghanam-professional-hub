Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026
Developer: Mohammed Elghanam
Contact: Mohammed.elghanam@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application Mohammed Elghanam Professional Hub (also presented on Google Play as Mohammed Elghanam R&D Hub) handles information.

1. Purpose of the app

The app is a professional portfolio and document hub. It presents executive profile information, engineering projects, skills, patents and professional recognition, an online recognition gallery, PDF portfolio documents, and professional contact links.

2. Personal data collection

The app does not require account creation, sign-in, registration, or user profiles. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases, and the developer does not intentionally collect personal information through forms or background tracking.

3. Internet and online gallery content

The recognition gallery loads image content over HTTPS from the Mohammed Elghanam Professional Hub website. As with normal web delivery, the hosting infrastructure may process standard technical request information such as IP address, browser or device request headers, request time, and security-related logs. This technical processing is used for content delivery, reliability, and security and is not used by the app to create advertising or behavioral profiles.

4. External links and communications

The app can open email, telephone, LinkedIn, and website links using the device's external applications. If you choose to use one of these actions, the external service or application may receive information according to its own privacy policy and settings. The Professional Hub does not control the privacy practices of those external services.

5. PDF viewing, saving, and sharing

Portfolio PDF files included with the app can be viewed locally. If you choose to save a PDF, it is written to a location selected or managed by the Android file system. If you choose to share a PDF, Android's share mechanism sends the file only to the application or recipient you select. The Professional Hub does not upload saved or shared PDF files to a developer-operated account or user database.

6. Device permissions

The Android application uses Internet access to load online gallery content and open web resources. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call-log, or broad background tracking permissions as part of its normal functionality.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally maintain a database of personal user information, there is no app account data retained by the developer for deletion. Technical hosting logs, where generated by infrastructure providers, are subject to the applicable provider's operational and retention practices.

8. Security

Online content is requested using HTTPS. The app is distributed as a signed Android application. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable technical measures are used for the app's intended professional-portfolio purpose.

9. Children

The app is intended as a professional and business portfolio. It is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not provide social networking, chat, or child-directed services.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, third-party services, or legal and platform requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to Mohammed.elghanam@gmail.com.

Privacy summary: no account, no ads, no analytics, no intentional personal-data collection, and no background location or contact access.
